# Understanding Brand Payments

This document explains how the payment totals in your Brand Dashboard are calculated. Our goal is to ensure you always see the **actual total cost** you will pay, with no hidden surprises.

## What's Included in the Total?

Whenever you see a "Total Paid," "Pending Payment," or "Upcoming Payment" amount, it includes four distinct parts:

1.  **Ambassador Commission:** The base reward earned by the athlete or creator for the sale, click, or lead.
2.  **Platform Fee:** A service fee (typically 4% for sales or 20% for other types) that covers the cost of running the sbonssy platform.
3.  **VAT (Value Added Tax):**
    - This only applies if the Ambassador is registered for VAT in **Finland**.
    - If applicable, we add the current Finnish VAT rate (25.5%) to the ambassador's part of the payment.
4.  **Stripe Processing Fee:** The transaction fee charged by Stripe to process your credit card or bank payment.

## How We Know Stripe Fees Before You Pay

For **Upcoming Payments**, we use a predictable "tiered" model to estimate the Stripe fee. This allows you to see exactly what the final invoice will look like before it's even generated.

| Monthly Total (EUR) | Estimated Stripe Fee    |
| :------------------ | :---------------------- |
| Up to €1,000        | 2.0% + €0.30            |
| €1,001 to €3,000    | 1.9% + €0.25            |
| €3,001 to €5,000    | 1.7% + €0.25            |
| Over €5,000         | No extra Stripe charges |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is the "Sale Amount" in the detailed records the same as the "Commission Paid"?

In the current "Detailed Payment Records" view, both columns represent the **Total Cost to the Brand** for that specific transaction. This ensures that when you sum up the rows, they match your bank statements and invoices exactly.

### Do I pay VAT on every transaction?

No. You only pay VAT if the ambassador you are working with is a tax-registered business in Finland. Most international or private individual ambassadors do not require a VAT addition from our Finnish entity.

### Are these totals final?

Yes. The dashboard reflects the "Gross Amount" (the final price), so the number you see is the number you will eventually see on your invoice and bank statement.
